Game Guide

How to Play

Take care of your dragon by feeding, petting, and letting it sleep.

  • Hunger: Decreases over time. If it hits 0, your dragon stops growing!
  • Happiness: Increases when you pet your dragon. Determines evolution paths.
  • Energy: Used for actions. Recover by sleeping.

Evolution Guide

Keep happiness above 50% for good evolutions!
